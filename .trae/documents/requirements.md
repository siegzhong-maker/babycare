# Product Requirements Document - Baby Care AI Platform

## 1. Project Overview
A comprehensive baby care platform leveraging AI to assist new parents with daily parenting challenges, combining tools, community, and expert knowledge.

## 2. Core Features

### 2.1 AI Parenting Assistants (MVP Priority)
- **Cry Translator**: Analyze baby's cry to suggest needs (Hungry, Sleepy, Diaper, Colic).
- **Poop Analyzer**: Visual analysis of baby's stool to provide health insights.
- **Smart Recipe Generator**: Generate personalized recipes based on available ingredients and baby's age/allergies.

### 2.2 Growth & Health Tracking
- **Baby Profile**: Manage multiple babies' profiles (Name, DOB, Gender).
- **Health Records**: Track allergies, preferences.

### 2.3 Knowledge & Community
- **Recipe Library**: Browse and search verified recipes.
- **Favorites**: Save favorite recipes.

## 3. User Roles
- **Guest**: Can view public content (limited).
- **Registered User**: Can use AI tools, track growth, save favorites.
- **Admin**: Manage content and users.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance**: Fast loading, especially for AI tool results.
- **Privacy**: Secure handling of baby photos and health data.
- **Responsiveness**: Mobile-first design for easy one-handed use.
